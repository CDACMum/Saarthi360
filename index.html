Features

Everything you need, in one place

Practice, code, revise and track your progress — and see exactly what to do next.

Exam-pattern MCQ practice

40-question timed sets per module, a question navigator, mark-for-review, and a result page that shows your weak topics and lets you revise only what you missed.

Live coding arena

Write and run code in the browser, test with your own input, and submit against hidden test cases — with XP, levels and streaks.

PythonJavaSQLMongoDBCQLLinuxPySpark

Chakra — AI study buddy

Explains any concept, walks you through your test mistakes and coding errors, quizzes you on weak topics, and talks in English or Hinglish.

Contests & leaderboard

Monthly challenges and a batch leaderboard that combines MCQ and coding — climb the weekly league and keep your streak alive.

Verifiable credentials

Each month your coordinator certifies your progress. Download the PDF, share a live verification link, or add it to LinkedIn in one click.

Fair-play exams

Full-screen tests with tab-switch and paste monitoring, server-side answer keys and shuffled options — so every score means something.
